FAQ

What can I sell?

Subscriptions, services and digital codes. You manage your catalog, pricing and products.

Do I need a Telegram bot?

Yes. Your Telegram bot is your customer-facing store. Connect your store bot during setup — or test without one using the store preview.

How does a supplier catalog work?

Connect a supplier account, choose your profit and enable products for sale. Supplier pricing and availability can change.

How do customers pay?

Customers top up their USDT wallet (Binance Pay, crypto networks, Telegram Stars, or a deposit code) and pay from their balance.
